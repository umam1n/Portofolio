📊 Featured Project: Strategic Workload Analysis (WLA)

Problem: High turnover in high-pressure departments despite "normal" staffing levels.

Methodology: A dual Top-Down Productivity Analysis and Bottom-Up WLA based on Mercer & Better&Co standards.
Key Insights & Visuals

    The Managerial Trap: Visualized how Leads in Operations were spending 86% of their time on execution rather than strategy.

    Capacity Mapping: Identified "Red Zones" where employees were mathematically over capacity (>1.15 FTE).

    Strategic Fix: Designed a delegation matrix that optimized Lead FTE from 1.36 to 1.05 by redistributing routine tasks.
